Ang Aming Simula

Ang Pangako ng Apo sa Kanyang Lola

Every Filipino family has that one beloved matriarch whose kitchen smelled of caramelized muscovado, toasty native garlic, and warm afternoon love. Para sa amin, si Apung Paning iyon.

What started as an apo's simple promise to make a Lola’s lifelong culinary dream come true has blossomed into Apung Paning Homemade Food Products. In every pouch, we preserve the authentic *gawang-bahay* soul while elevating traditional snacks into modern gourmet delicacies you’ll be proud to gift to colleagues, barkada, and balikbayans.

The Gourmet Merienda Lineup

Crafted for mindful afternoon breaks, university study sessions, and midnight snack cravings.

Cinnamon-Coffee

A comforting morning hug in snack form. Sweet cinnamon warmth harmonized with deep roasted barako aroma.

Best Paired With: Hot Kapeng Barako, iced oat latte, or afternoon study notes.

Dark Cacao Sea Salt

Bittersweet tablea richness with mineral crystals of sea salt that cut through the natural sweetness of saba bananas.

Best Paired With: Midnight movie watch parties, red wine, or vanilla bean ice cream topping.

Spicy Garlic Cornick & Peanuts

Super crisp roasted corn kernels, toasted peanuts, and whole crispy garlic chips laced with mild chili flakes.

Best Paired With: Ice-cold soda, craft beer, or road trips along the scenic Laguna highway.
