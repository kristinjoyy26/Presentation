Have Questions?

Common questions,
answered honestly.

If you can't find what you're looking for here, feel free to reach out directly.

Do you charge hourly or by project?

I work on monthly retainers for ongoing management, and fixed project quotes for one-off strategy or campaign work. You'll always know the number before we start — no surprise hourly overages.

How long have you been doing social media management?

I've been working professionally in social media, campaigns, and digital support for 5+ years, across corporate accounts, e-commerce platforms, and freelance client work. It's a craft I've refined with both hands-on experience and continuous learning of new tools and platform changes.

Can you run a trial campaign before I commit?

I don't offer free trial work, but you're welcome to review my case studies and past results to get a strong sense of my process and quality. I'm also happy to walk you through exactly how I'd approach your account on a discovery call.

What's the process, and how long does it take to get started?

I start with a discovery call to understand your goals, audience, and current channels. From audit to a live strategy, it usually takes 1–2 weeks depending on scope — then we move into ongoing execution and reporting.

Which platforms and tools do you work with?

I run paid ads across Meta, TikTok, and Google, and manage organic content across all major social platforms. On the tooling side, I work daily in Metricool, Canva, CapCut, Notion, Shopify, Slack, and more — see the full toolkit above.
